# FX University — Forex Trading Course

## What this course is

FX University is a structured, self-paced Forex education program for
complete beginners who want to actually understand the market — not just
collect scattered tips from free videos. It takes a student with little or
no background in Forex through ten modules, in order, ending with a
student who can read a chart, apply basic technical and fundamental
analysis, manage risk properly, write a personal trading plan, and run a
backtesting and journaling process to keep improving on their own.

This course is educational. It does not manage money, does not provide
signals, and does not offer personalized financial advice. It does not
promise profitability, a guaranteed win rate, or a specific income. Every
module is built around building real understanding and a repeatable
process instead.

## The learning framework

Every module in this course follows the same underlying progression:

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Learn** | Understand the concept in plain language before touching the terminology. |
| **Understand** | Connect the concept to how the market actually behaves, with real, worked examples. |
| **Practice** | Apply the concept through exercises, not just by reading about it. |
| **Backtest** | Test ideas against historical price data before ever risking real money. |
| **Journal** | Record what actually happened — honestly — instead of relying on memory or feeling. |
| **Improve** | Use what the journal and backtests show to refine the process, one small change at a time. |

Modules 1–5 build the *Learn* and *Understand* stages (market mechanics,
charts, technical analysis, price action, fundamentals). Modules 6–7 build
the discipline layer (risk management, psychology) that makes everything
before it usable. Modules 8–10 build the *Practice → Backtest → Journal →
Improve* loop that turns knowledge into a repeatable process.

## Who this course is for

- Complete beginners who want to learn Forex properly, from the start.
- People who've tried trading before but never had a structured path.
- Traders who struggle with consistency because they never built a real
  process.
- Anyone who wants to understand the market instead of blindly following
  signals or copying other traders.

## Who this course is not for

- Anyone looking for guaranteed profits or a get-rich-quick system.
- Anyone expecting instant results with no study or practice.
- Anyone who wants someone else to trade on their behalf.
- Anyone unwilling to spend time on the fundamentals before moving to
  strategy.

## Risk disclosure

Forex trading involves substantial risk, including the potential loss of
capital. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. Past performance —
including backtested results — does not guarantee future outcomes. No
strategy, indicator, or course can guarantee profits. Nothing in this
course is personalized financial or investment advice. Students should
fully understand these risks, and are strongly encouraged to practice on
a demo account, before committing real capital, and should confirm the
regulatory status of any broker they consider using.

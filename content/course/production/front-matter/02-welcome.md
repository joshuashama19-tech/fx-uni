# Welcome to FX University

Welcome. FX University is a structured, ten-module Forex education
program built for beginners — people who want to actually understand
how the market works, rather than collect scattered tips from wherever
they happen to land next.

## What you'll learn

Across the ten modules, you'll build a complete process: how the Forex
market actually functions, how to read a chart, how to apply technical
and price-action analysis, how fundamental events move currency pairs,
how to manage risk properly, how to manage the psychological side of
trading, how to write your own complete trading plan, how to test that
plan against evidence and track your own results honestly, and how to
apply all of it responsibly in the real world. Each module builds
directly on the ones before it.

## How the course progresses

The ten modules are designed to be worked through **in order**. Later
modules assume you've completed the earlier ones — Module 8's trading
plan draws on the analysis tools from Modules 2–5 and the risk and
psychology rules from Modules 6–7; Module 9's testing process assumes
that plan exists; Module 10 assumes you've built and tested it. Skipping
ahead means working with tools you haven't actually built yet.

## Why the course is structured this way

Trading decisions rest on several different skills at once — reading a
chart, sizing a position, managing your own reaction to a loss, and
sticking to a written plan are all different skills, and each one is
genuinely harder to learn out of order. Building them one at a time,
each on a solid foundation, is what this course is designed around.

## Study, practice, backtest, journal, review

Every module in this course follows the same underlying framework:

```
LEARN → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE → BACKTEST → JOURNAL → IMPROVE
```

You **learn** a concept in plain language before its formal terminology.
You **understand** it through real, worked examples. You **practice** it
through exercises, not just by reading about it. You **backtest** ideas
against historical evidence before ever risking real money. You
**journal** what actually happens, honestly, instead of relying on
memory or feeling. And you **improve** by using what the backtests and
journal show you, one deliberate change at a time. This isn't a slogan —
it's the literal order Modules 1 through 10 are built in, and it's the
same cycle you'll keep using for as long as you trade, long after this
course ends.

## How to approach this course

Take the modules in order. Read each lesson before moving to the next
one. Do the exercises — they're where the concepts actually become
skills. Take each knowledge checkpoint honestly, without checking the
answer key first. Use the worksheets and checklists as you go, not just
as something to skim. And expect this to take real time and real
practice — this course is built to teach a genuine process, not a
shortcut.

# Course Complete

*Production note: this page follows Module 10's Completion Checklist in the
student-facing sequence — it is the final page of the core curriculum,
before the Practical Resource Library. Its language is drawn directly from
Module 10's own approved Key Takeaways, "Before You Move On," and Lesson 15

---

You've worked through the entire structured FX University curriculum —
from basic market mechanics in Module 1 through a complete, testable,
responsibly-applied trading process in Module 10. That's thirteen to
fifteen lessons in every one of ten modules, each with its own exercises,
knowledge checkpoint, and completion checklist, plus a full library of
worksheets and templates built for real use, not just reading.

## The progression you've completed

```
FOUNDATION → ANALYSIS → RISK → PSYCHOLOGY → PLAN → TEST → JOURNAL → DEVELOP
```

- **Foundation** (Module 1) — how the forex market actually works: pairs,
  pips, lots, orders, leverage, sessions, brokers.
- **Analysis** (Modules 2–5) — reading charts and candles, technical
  analysis, price action, and fundamental analysis.
- **Risk** (Module 6) — position sizing, expectancy, drawdown, and capital
  preservation.
- **Psychology** (Module 7) — the mental and behavioral side of trading:
  fear, greed, discipline, and consistency.
- **Plan** (Module 8) — turning everything learned into one written,
  personal trading plan.
- **Test** (Module 9, backtesting half) — testing that plan against
  historical data before risking anything on it.
- **Journal** (Module 9, journaling half) — recording and reviewing real
  results honestly, over time.
- **Develop** (Module 10) — applying all of it responsibly: demo practice,
  broker and scam due diligence, execution discipline, performance review,
  and a personal development roadmap for what comes next.

## What this means — and what it doesn't

Completing this checklist means you've built, in writing, your own trading
plan, backtested it against historical data, and have a journal and review
process ready to use going forward. It means you understand the market
mechanics, the analysis methods, the risk math, the psychological
pressures, and the practical safety steps this course covers.

It does not mean trading success, profitability, or income are guaranteed,
and it does not make you a professional trader. As Module 10 puts it
directly: *finishing this course does not make someone a professional
trader; it gives a complete, honest process to keep learning, testing, and
improving from.* No module in this course claims otherwise, and this page
doesn't either.

## What comes next

The cycle this course teaches doesn't end here:

```
LEARN → UNDERSTAND → PRACTICE → BACKTEST → JOURNAL → IMPROVE
```

Every stage in that chain — including the last one — is something a
trader keeps doing for as long as they trade. Module 10's Lesson 15 laid
out a practical 90-day starting structure (foundations into practice,
then consistency and review, then evidence and decision) for putting this
course's process to work. Whatever pace you take from here, the same
pattern applies: keep practicing deliberately, keep testing before
changing anything, keep recording results honestly, and let accumulated
evidence — not a single trade, a single week, or a fixed calendar — guide
what you do next.

The full set of worksheets and templates referenced throughout the course
is compiled in the Practical Resource Library that follows this page, so
you have everything in one place to keep using.

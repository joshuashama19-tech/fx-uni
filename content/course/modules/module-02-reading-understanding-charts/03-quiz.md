# Module 2 — Knowledge Checkpoint

15 questions covering everything in Module 2. Mixed format: multiple
choice, true/false, short answer, and chart-reading scenarios. Write
down your answers before checking `04-answer-key.md` — checking as you
go defeats the point of a checkpoint.

**1. Multiple choice.** What does a single candlestick represent on a
chart?
A) A prediction of the next period's price
B) A summary of the open, high, low, and close for one fixed period of
   time
C) The average price over the entire chart
D) A single executed trade

**2. Short answer.** In your own words, define each part of OHLC.

**3. Calculation.** A 1-hour EUR/USD candle shows: Open 1.09400, High
1.09460, Low 1.09300, Close 1.09330. Is this candle bullish or bearish?
Calculate its body size in pips.

**4. Calculation.** Using the same candle as Question 3, calculate its
upper wick, lower wick, and full range, all in pips.

**5. True or False.** A single bullish candle guarantees that price will
continue rising afterward.

**6. Chart-reading scenario.** You see three consecutive 1-hour candles:
a large bullish candle, followed by a small-bodied candle near the top
of the first candle's range, followed by another small-bodied candle in
the same area. What does this sequence suggest is happening, and what
should you watch for on the next candle?

**7. Short answer.** You switch a chart from a 1-hour timeframe to a
daily timeframe. What changes about what you can see, and what changes
about what you can't see?

**8. Multiple choice.** A market's swing highs are recorded, in order,
as 1.31200 → 1.31450 → 1.31700, and its swing lows as 1.30800 → 1.31050
→ 1.31300. What does this describe?
A) A downtrend
B) A range
C) An uptrend
D) A false breakout

**9. True or False.** A range is defined by swing highs and lows that
form a clear, sustained directional sequence.

**10. Short answer.** What is a support level, and why does a price
level tend to become support or resistance in the first place?

**11. Multiple choice.** Which of the following best describes a false
breakout?
A) Price closes several candles beyond a level and keeps moving in that
   direction
B) Price never approaches a support or resistance level at all
C) Price moves beyond a level, appears to break it, then reverses back
   to the other side
D) Price stays within a range indefinitely

**12. True or False.** A candle closing beyond a support or resistance
level guarantees that price will continue in that direction.

**13. Short answer.** In your own words, explain what "market structure"
means and what it's built from.

**14. Chart-reading scenario.** The daily chart for a pair shows a clear,
sustained uptrend. The 15-minute chart for the last few hours shows a
short sequence of lower highs and lower lows. Explain how you would
interpret these two charts together, and why looking at only one of them
could be misleading.

**15. Multiple choice.** Which of the following is a common beginner
chart-reading mistake covered in this module?
A) Checking more than one timeframe before drawing a conclusion
B) Treating a support/resistance zone as an approximate area rather than
   an exact price
C) Assuming any move beyond a level is automatically a valid breakout
D) Reading a sequence of candles together instead of in isolation

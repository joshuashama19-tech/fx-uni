# Module 2 — Completion Checklist

## Before You Move On

Go through this honestly — check each box only if you could actually do
it right now, without flipping back to the lesson. Module 3 builds
analysis tools directly on top of this skill; it doesn't re-teach it.

### Core understanding

- [ ] I can read a basic candlestick and identify its open, high, low,
      and close.
- [ ] I can explain OHLC in my own words.
- [ ] I can tell whether a candle is bullish or bearish, and I know that
      color alone doesn't tell me how strong or meaningful the move was.
- [ ] I can calculate a candle's body, upper wick, lower wick, and range
      from its OHLC values.
- [ ] I can read a short sequence of candles and describe the story it
      tells, not just list each candle's color in isolation.

### Timeframes and trend

- [ ] I understand what a timeframe is, and what changes (and what's
      lost) when I switch between a lower and a higher timeframe.
- [ ] I can identify an uptrend by checking for higher highs and higher
      lows.
- [ ] I can identify a downtrend by checking for lower highs and lower
      lows.
- [ ] I can recognize a range — and I don't force every chart into
      "uptrend" or "downtrend" when it's genuinely just moving sideways.

### Levels and structure

- [ ] I can identify a potential support or resistance level by looking
      for a price area where the market has reacted more than once.
- [ ] I understand support/resistance as approximate zones, not exact
      single prices.
- [ ] I can explain what a breakout is, and why a breakout — even a
      convincing one — is never a guarantee that price will keep moving
      in that direction.
- [ ] I understand what a false breakout is, and that genuine and false
      breakouts often look identical while they're happening.
- [ ] I can describe, at a basic level, what "market structure" means:
      trend direction, key levels, and whether they've held or broken.

### Multi-timeframe analysis

- [ ] I understand what multi-timeframe analysis is and why checking
      more than one timeframe can change — or complete — the picture a
      single chart gives me.
- [ ] I can walk through a basic example of a higher-timeframe trend and
      a lower-timeframe pullback, and explain how they fit together.

### Practice completed

- [ ] I completed all 11 exercises in `02-exercises.md`.
- [ ] I completed the 15-question checkpoint in `03-quiz.md` before
      looking at the answers.
- [ ] I checked my answers against `04-answer-key.md` and re-read any
      lesson connected to a question I missed.

### Mindset check

- [ ] I understand that chart reading describes what has already
      happened and what may be more or less likely — never what is
      certain. No pattern, trend, level, or breakout in this module
      guarantees future price movement.
- [ ] I understand that this module taught me to *read* a chart, not to
      trade off it — the analysis tools, price-action methodology, and
      risk management that turn this into a decision-making process come
      in later modules.

---

If every box above is checked honestly, you're ready for **Module 3 —
Technical Analysis**. If anything is still unclear, that's a normal part
of learning this material — go back to the relevant lesson in
`01-content.md` before moving forward. There's no benefit to continuing
on a shaky foundation.

# Module 10 — Knowledge Checkpoint

18 questions covering everything in Module 10. Mixed format: multiple
choice, true/false, short answer, and scenario-based questions. Write
down your answers before checking `04-answer-key.md` — checking as you go
defeats the point of a checkpoint.

**1. Multiple choice.** Per Lesson 1, why is conceptual knowledge alone
not sufficient before trading real money?

A) Because knowledge becomes outdated after a few months
B) Because executing a plan consistently, under real conditions, is a
   separate skill from understanding it
C) Because this course's content is intentionally incomplete
D) Because demo trading is required by all brokers

**2. True or False.** Demo trading guarantees that a trader is ready for
live trading.

**3. Short answer.** Name at least three items from Lesson 2's Demo
Trading Readiness Checklist.

**4. Scenario.** A trader is about to start a trading session. Per
Lesson 3, name two things they should do *before* the session begins.

**5. Multiple choice.** Per Lesson 4, this course treats broker
regulation as:

A) An absolute guarantee against every possible problem
B) Irrelevant, since all brokers carry the same risk
C) A meaningful factor, but not a complete guarantee on its own
D) Something that only matters for large accounts

**6. Short answer.** Per Lesson 5, why is checking a broker's own website
badge insufficient to verify a regulatory claim? What should be checked
instead?

**7. Scenario.** An offer promises a guaranteed 10% monthly return with
"zero risk." Per Lesson 6, name at least two specific warning signs
present in this offer.

**8. True or False.** Per Lesson 6, a professional-looking website and
active social media presence are, by themselves, reliable evidence that
a company is legitimate.

**9. Short answer.** Per Lesson 7, explain the difference between
learning from a signal provider's reasoning and simply copying their
entries.

**10. Multiple choice.** Per Lesson 7, this course's position on using
trading signals is:

A) Signals must never be used under any circumstances
B) Signals should always be copied exactly, including position size
C) Any signal source can be evaluated using specific criteria, and
   should never replace independent understanding
D) Only licensed brokers are allowed to provide signals

**11. Short answer.** Per Lesson 8, name at least three elements that
commonly appear in a prop-firm evaluation program's rules.

**12. True or False.** Per Lesson 8, passing a prop-firm evaluation
proves that a trader has long-term profitable trading ability.

**13. Scenario.** A trader understands every concept in this course and
has a demo-tested plan, but the money they're considering trading with is
needed for next month's rent. Per Lesson 9, which kind of readiness is
missing?

**14. True or False.** This course states a specific number of demo days
or trades that guarantees a trader is ready for live trading.

**15. Short answer.** Name at least four items from Lesson 10's Execution
Checklist.

**16. Scenario.** A trader's monthly results are profitable overall, but
their journal shows that a third of their trades didn't meet their
written setup's required conditions. Per Lesson 11, is a profitable
result enough to call this a well-executed month? Explain.

**17. Short answer.** Per Lesson 12, explain the difference between
evidence-based improvement and abandoning a strategy after one losing
trade.

**18. True or False.** Per Lesson 15, this course recommends that every
student automatically begin live trading after 90 days of practice.

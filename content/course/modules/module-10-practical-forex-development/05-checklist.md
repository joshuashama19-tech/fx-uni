# Module 10 — Completion Checklist

## Before You Move On

Go through this honestly — check each box only if you could actually do
it right now, not just recall reading about it. This is the final
checklist in FX University's core curriculum.

### Application and practice

- [ ] I understand the transition from learning to application, and why
      knowledge alone isn't sufficient for consistent execution.
- [ ] I understand what demo trading is and what it can and cannot show
      about live-trading readiness.
- [ ] I have completed my own Demo Trading Readiness Checklist honestly.
- [ ] I can apply my trading plan through a complete before/during/after
      session structure.

### Broker and platform safety

- [ ] I understand what to look for when evaluating a broker.
- [ ] I understand broker due diligence, and I can verify a regulatory
      claim independently against the relevant regulator's own register,
      rather than trusting a website badge.

### Fraud and dependency awareness

- [ ] I can identify the major warning signs of a Forex scam.
- [ ] I understand the risks of signal dependency, and how to evaluate a
      signal provider rather than following one blindly.
- [ ] I understand what prop-firm evaluation programs generally involve,
      and that specific program rules can and do change.

### Readiness and execution

- [ ] I understand the difference between knowledge readiness, process
      readiness, and financial readiness.
- [ ] I understand common execution risks in real trading conditions and
      how a pre-trade checklist helps reduce them.

### Review and improvement

- [ ] I can perform a structured performance review connecting to my
      Module 9 journal.
- [ ] I understand the continuous-improvement cycle, and the difference
      between evidence-based change and reacting to a single trade.
- [ ] I have a personal development roadmap, and I honestly know which
      stage my own progress is currently at.
- [ ] I have written my own 90-day development plan.

### Practice completed

- [ ] I completed all 14 exercises in `02-exercises.md`.
- [ ] I completed the 18-question checkpoint in `03-quiz.md` before
      looking at the answers.
- [ ] I checked my answers against `04-answer-key.md` and re-read any
      lesson connected to a question I missed.

### Final mindset check

- [ ] I understand that completing this course does not make me a
      professional trader, and does not guarantee profitable trading.
- [ ] I understand the complete course process, end to end: Learn →
      Understand → Practice → Backtest → Journal → Improve — and that
      this cycle continues for as long as I trade.

---

**Course Complete — Learn → Understand → Practice → Backtest → Journal →
Improve.**

If every box above is checked honestly, you've worked through the entire
FX University core curriculum — from basic market mechanics in Module 1
through a complete, tested, responsibly-applied trading process here in
Module 10. If anything is still unclear, that's a normal part of
learning material this dense — go back to the relevant lesson, in this
module or an earlier one, before treating any stage as finished. What
happens from here is the cycle itself, continued: practicing, reviewing,
and improving, for as long as you keep trading.

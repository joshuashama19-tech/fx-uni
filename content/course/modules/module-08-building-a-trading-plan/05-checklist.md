# Module 8 — Completion Checklist

## Before You Move On

Go through this honestly — check each box only if you've actually written
the relevant section, not just read about it. Module 9 assumes you have a
complete, written first-draft trading plan ready to test.

### Core understanding

- [ ] I understand what a trading plan is, and how it differs from a
      strategy and from a single trading idea.
- [ ] I have selected a trading style suited to my own time, schedule,
      and experience.
- [ ] I have defined my markets, using the Market Selection Worksheet.
- [ ] I have defined my timeframes, using the Context → Setup → Entry
      framework.

### The written plan itself

- [ ] I have defined my setup, in observable, objective terms, with
      required conditions separated from optional confluences.
- [ ] I have written entry rules, including a specific entry trigger.
- [ ] I have written stop-loss rules, tied to structural invalidation
      rather than an arbitrary distance.
- [ ] I have written exit rules, including my take-profit method.
- [ ] I have written risk rules, including a specific maximum risk per
      trade.
- [ ] I have written news rules, stating how my plan handles scheduled
      economic releases.
- [ ] I have defined my trading schedule, covering before, during, and
      after each session.
- [ ] I have defined no-trade conditions, specific enough to check in the
      moment.
- [ ] I have written psychology/behavior rules, connecting to Module 7.

### Assembly and review

- [ ] I have a complete trading-plan template, with all fourteen sections
      filled in.
- [ ] I have audited my plan using the Final Trading Plan Audit, and
      addressed anything I couldn't honestly check off.
- [ ] I understand that my plan is a first draft, not a proven system,
      and that it still needs testing.

### Practice completed

- [ ] I completed all 14 exercises in `02-exercises.md`.
- [ ] I completed the 17-question checkpoint in `03-quiz.md` before
      looking at the answers.
- [ ] I checked my answers against `04-answer-key.md` and re-read any
      lesson connected to a question I missed.

### Mindset check

- [ ] I understand that no single trading style, market list, or setup
      definition is presented in this module as universally correct.
- [ ] I understand that this module does not claim a written trading
      plan guarantees profitable trading.
- [ ] I understand the difference between a plan that's clearly written
      and a plan that's actually been tested and proven.

---

**Ready for Module 9 — Backtesting & Trading Journal.**

If every box above is checked honestly, you're ready to move forward. If
anything is still unclear or incomplete, that's a normal part of building
a first trading plan — go back to the relevant lesson in `01-content.md`
before continuing. Module 9 takes the plan you've just written and
teaches you how to actually test it against evidence.

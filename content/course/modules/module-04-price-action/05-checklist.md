# Module 4 — Completion Checklist

## Before You Move On

Go through this honestly — check each box only if you could actually do
it right now, without flipping back to the lesson. Module 5 builds on
this module and doesn't re-teach it; Module 6 covers the risk-management
math this module deliberately left conceptual.

### Core understanding

- [ ] I can explain what price action is, and how it differs from
      indicator-based analysis, without claiming it's more certain.
- [ ] I can read a sequence of candles and describe what price is doing
      directly, separating what I actually observe from how I interpret
      it.
- [ ] I can define market structure, and I can define a swing high and a
      swing low precisely.
- [ ] I can identify swing highs and swing lows on a described chart, and
      I know not every small wiggle counts as one.

### Structure, zones, and liquidity

- [ ] I can classify structure as bullish, bearish, or ranging from a
      sequence of swing points, checking both the highs *and* the lows.
- [ ] I can identify a supply or demand area from a description, and I
      can explain why these are zones of interest, not guaranteed
      reversal points.
- [ ] I do not claim to know exactly where "institutions" placed orders,
      and I don't treat supply/demand zones as a magic, guaranteed
      mechanism.
- [ ] I can explain what a liquidity pool is and what equal highs/lows
      are, and I can describe a liquidity sweep as an observation without
      overclaiming certainty about who caused it or why.

### Structural shifts

- [ ] I can identify a Break of Structure (BOS) and explain what it
      confirms.
- [ ] I can identify a Change of Character (CHOCH) and explain what it
      suggests — and I can clearly state what it does **not** prove.
- [ ] I understand that swing-point and BOS/CHOCH terminology varies
      between traders and educators, and that this course's definitions
      are a consistent convention, not the only correct one.

### Entries, stops, and targets

- [ ] I can explain the difference between a confirmation entry and an
      anticipation entry, and I understand neither is universally
      "correct."
- [ ] I can describe what a defined entry trigger is, and why deciding it
      in advance protects against emotional entries.
- [ ] I can explain why a stop loss belongs at the point that invalidates
      the trade idea, not at an arbitrary fixed distance.
- [ ] I can calculate risk in pips, reward in pips, and a risk-to-reward
      ratio from an entry, stop loss, and target — and I can explain why
      a high ratio alone doesn't guarantee profitability.

### Multi-timeframe analysis and framework

- [ ] I can describe the higher-timeframe-context /
      middle-timeframe-structure / lower-timeframe-execution framework,
      and I understand it's one possible approach, not a universal rule.
- [ ] I can describe at least one reasonable way to handle timeframes
      that disagree with each other, instead of only trusting whichever
      one agrees with what I already want to do.
- [ ] I can list the ten steps of the repeatable price-action framework
      from Lesson 12, in roughly the right order.

### Practice completed

- [ ] I completed all 12 exercises in `02-exercises.md`.
- [ ] I completed the 15-question checkpoint in `03-quiz.md` before
      looking at the answers.
- [ ] I checked my answers against `04-answer-key.md` and re-read any
      lesson connected to a question I missed.

### Mindset check

- [ ] I understand that no structure reading, zone, BOS, CHOCH, or
      combination of them in this module guarantees a specific outcome,
      a winning trade, or knowledge of another participant's intent.
- [ ] I do not treat a CHOCH as confirmed proof of a reversal, and I
      don't treat more confluence between price-action signals as
      certainty.
- [ ] I understand that this module deliberately left position-sizing
      and risk-per-trade calculations for Module 6, and I have not tried
      to shortcut that with a rule of my own.

---

If every box above is checked honestly, you're ready for **Module 5 —
Fundamental Analysis**. If anything is still unclear, that's a normal
part of learning this material — go back to the relevant lesson in
`01-content.md` before moving forward. There's no benefit to continuing
on a shaky foundation.

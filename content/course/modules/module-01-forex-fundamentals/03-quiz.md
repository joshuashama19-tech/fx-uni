# Module 1 — Knowledge Checkpoint

15 questions covering everything in Module 1. Mixed format: multiple
choice, true/false, short answer, and practical calculations. Write down
your answers before checking `04-answer-key.md` — checking as you go
defeats the point of a checkpoint.

**1. Multiple choice.** In the pair USD/CHF, which currency is the base
currency?
A) US Dollar
B) Swiss Franc
C) Neither — Forex pairs don't have a base currency
D) Both, equally

**2. True or False.** Forex is traded through a single centralized
exchange, similar to a stock exchange.

**3. Multiple choice.** Which of the following is classified as a
**minor** pair (a cross)?
A) EUR/USD
B) USD/JPY
C) EUR/GBP
D) USD/TRY

**4. Short answer.** In your own words, explain the difference between
the bid price and the ask price.

**5. Calculation.** EUR/USD shows a bid of 1.09340 and an ask of
1.09354. What is the spread, in pips?

**6. Calculation.** USD/JPY moves from 148.220 to 148.610. How many pips
did it move?

**7. Multiple choice.** Approximately how much is one pip worth on 1
standard lot of a USD-quoted pair like EUR/USD?
A) $0.10
B) $1.00
C) $10.00
D) $100.00

**8. True or False.** A buy limit order is placed *above* the current
market price.

**9. Short answer.** A trader wants to enter a trade immediately, at
whatever price is currently available. Which order type should they use?

**10. Multiple choice.** What does leverage primarily allow a trader to
do?
A) Eliminate the risk of a trade
B) Control a larger position size than their deposited capital alone
   would allow
C) Guarantee a specific spread
D) Avoid needing a broker

**11. True or False.** Leverage magnifies potential losses in the same
proportion that it magnifies potential gains.

**12. Short answer.** Name the four major Forex trading sessions, and
state which overlap is generally considered the most active for major
pairs.

**13. Multiple choice.** Which group represents the largest share of
total Forex trading volume?
A) Retail traders
B) Commercial and investment banks
C) Individual investors
D) Small businesses paying overseas suppliers

**14. Calculation.** A trader with a $1,000 account opens 1 standard lot
of EUR/USD. A 20-pip adverse price move occurs (use the approximate pip
value of $10/standard lot). Approximately what dollar amount, and what
percentage of the account, did the trader lose on that move?

**15. Short answer.** Why does a broker's regulatory status matter in
practice — name at least one concrete reason, not just "it's safer."

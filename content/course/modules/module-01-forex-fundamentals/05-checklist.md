# Module 1 — Completion Checklist

## Before You Move On

Go through this honestly — check each box only if you could actually do
it right now, without flipping back to the lesson. This isn't a
formality; Module 2 (Reading & Understanding Charts) assumes every item
below is second nature.

### Core understanding

- [ ] I can explain what the Forex market is and why it exists, in my
      own words.
- [ ] I can identify the base currency and the quote currency in any
      pair I'm shown.
- [ ] I can classify a pair as major, minor, or exotic, and explain why
      that classification matters.
- [ ] I can read a bid and an ask price and calculate the spread between
      them.
- [ ] I can calculate a pip movement correctly — including on a JPY
      pair, where the pip is a different decimal place.
- [ ] I understand what a standard, mini, and micro lot are, and roughly
      what one pip is worth on each.

### Practical mechanics

- [ ] I can explain the difference between a market order, a limit
      order, and a stop order, and pick the correct one for a given
      scenario.
- [ ] I can explain what leverage is and what margin is, and I
      understand that leverage magnifies both gains and losses — it
      doesn't reduce risk.
- [ ] I can calculate the approximate margin required for a simple
      position, given the leverage ratio.
- [ ] I can name the four major trading sessions and explain why the
      London/New York overlap matters.
- [ ] I understand, broadly, who actually participates in the Forex
      market and why that matters for how I interpret price movement.
- [ ] I understand what a broker actually does, and why checking a
      broker's regulation and terms is a real safety step.
- [ ] I can walk through, step by step, what happens from placing a
      trade to it closing — including setting a stop loss and a take
      profit before entering.

### Practice completed

- [ ] I completed all 11 exercises in `02-exercises.md`.
- [ ] I completed the 15-question checkpoint in `03-quiz.md` before
      looking at the answers.
- [ ] I checked my answers against `04-answer-key.md` and re-read any
      lesson connected to a question I missed.

### Mindset check

- [ ] I understand that nothing in this module — or this course —
      guarantees profitable trading, and I'm continuing because I want
      to actually understand the market, not because I expect
      guaranteed results.
- [ ] I understand that the terminology in this module (pips, lots,
      leverage, spread) represents real mechanics with real financial
      consequences, not just vocabulary to memorize.

---

If every box above is checked honestly, you're ready for **Module 2 —
Reading & Understanding Charts**. If anything is still unclear, that's a
normal part of learning this material — go back to the relevant lesson
in `01-content.md` before moving forward. There's no benefit to
continuing on a shaky foundation.

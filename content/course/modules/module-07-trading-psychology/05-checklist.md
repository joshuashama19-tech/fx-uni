# Module 7 — Completion Checklist

## Before You Move On

Go through this honestly — check each box only if you could actually do
it right now, without flipping back to the lesson. Module 8 assumes you
can already recognize these patterns and use these frameworks in your
own decision-making.

### Core understanding

- [ ] I understand what trading psychology means, and I can explain the
      difference between a losing trade and a bad decision.
- [ ] I can identify fear-driven behavior (hesitation, early exits,
      moved stops, avoiding valid setups) in a realistic scenario.
- [ ] I can identify greed-driven behavior (oversized positions, moved
      targets, excessive trades) in a realistic scenario.
- [ ] I understand what FOMO is, and why missing one setup is not the
      same as missing the market.
- [ ] I understand what revenge trading is, and why the next trade
      shouldn't be responsible for recovering the last one.
- [ ] I can recognize overtrading and impatience, and apply Lesson 6's
      practical filter for whether a setup deserves consideration.
- [ ] I understand confirmation bias and at least two other biases from
      Lesson 7, each with a practical countermeasure.

### Losses, discipline, and patience

- [ ] I can distinguish a planned loss from a careless, rule-violating
      loss.
- [ ] I understand discipline as a specific, checkable set of behaviors
      — not a fixed personality trait.
- [ ] I understand patience, and why no-trade periods are a normal,
      expected part of trading with real criteria.

### Practical tools

- [ ] I have a Pre-Trade Psychology Check I actually intend to use before
      entering trades.
- [ ] I have a Post-Trade Review process I actually intend to use after
      closing trades.
- [ ] I have identified my own personal emotional triggers, honestly,
      using Exercise 11's trigger map.
- [ ] I have written personal psychology rules, specific to my own
      tendencies, not copied from the module's examples.
- [ ] I understand how winning and losing streaks each affect behavior
      (overconfidence and fear, respectively), and why neither should
      change my risk or process on its own.

### Practice completed

- [ ] I completed all 14 exercises in `02-exercises.md`.
- [ ] I completed the 17-question checkpoint in `03-quiz.md` before
      looking at the answers.
- [ ] I checked my answers against `04-answer-key.md` and re-read any
      lesson connected to a question I missed.

### Mindset check

- [ ] I understand that this module does not guarantee profitable
      trading, and that mastering psychology does not remove the
      uncertainty every trade carries.
- [ ] I understand that emotion cannot simply be eliminated — the goal is
      recognizing it and having a process that reduces the avoidable
      mistakes it can cause.
- [ ] I understand that consistency of process, not consistency of
      results, is the actual measure of discipline.

If every box above is checked honestly, you're ready to move forward. If

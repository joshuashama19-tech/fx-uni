# Module 9 — Completion Checklist

## Before You Move On

Go through this honestly — check each box only if you could actually do
it right now, not just recall reading about it. Module 10 assumes you
have a working testing-and-journaling process ready to apply, not just a
memory of the concepts.

### Core understanding

- [ ] I understand what backtesting is, and I can explain what it can and
      cannot prove about a strategy's future performance.
- [ ] I can prepare a strategy's rules so they're specific and testable,
      not vague or judgment-based.
- [ ] I can choose an appropriate historical data source and period,
      including more than one type of market condition.
- [ ] I can manually backtest a strategy step by step, moving forward one
      candle at a time and avoiding look-ahead bias.
- [ ] I understand the R-multiple concept and can record backtested
      trades in a Backtesting Log.

### Measuring results

- [ ] I can calculate win rate from a set of backtested results.
- [ ] I can calculate average win and average loss.
- [ ] I can calculate expectancy per trade.
- [ ] I can calculate profit factor.
- [ ] I can read a simple equity curve to find a backtest's largest
      drawdown and longest losing streak.

### Statistical caution and biases

- [ ] I understand why no single trade count makes a backtest "proven,"
      and why a small sample can look outstanding or terrible by chance.
- [ ] I can recognize at least five of Lesson 10's nine backtesting
      biases and errors when described in a scenario.
- [ ] I have honestly checked my own backtesting process against the
      Bias/Error Checklist.

### Journaling and review

- [ ] I have a Trading Journal Template ready to use for every future
      trade, covering before, during, and after each one.
- [ ] I understand the Trade Review Framework's four questions and can
      apply them to a completed trade.
- [ ] I have a Weekly Review Template ready to use.
- [ ] I have a Monthly Review Template ready to use.
- [ ] I understand the Change → Retest → Compare → Decide cycle, and why
      only one change should be tested at a time.

### Practice completed

- [ ] I completed all 15 exercises in `02-exercises.md`.
- [ ] I completed the 18-question checkpoint in `03-quiz.md` before
      looking at the answers.
- [ ] I checked my answers against `04-answer-key.md` and re-read any
      lesson connected to a question I missed.

### Mindset check

- [ ] I understand that completing this module's backtest and journal
      setup is the start of an ongoing process, not proof that any
      strategy is proven or ready for live trading — and that forward
      testing on a demo account, journaling every trade, comes next.
